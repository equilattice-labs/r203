# TabAlpha Business Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Market:** English-speaking global users  
**Network:** Robinhood Chain mainnet

## 1. Executive Summary

TabAlpha is the social investing layer for Robinhood Chain. It transforms market insight from creators, analysts, and communities into transparent, rules-based, followable onchain strategies called **Tabs**.

Today, investors discover ideas on X, validate them across fragmented data products, manually recreate trades in a separate application, and then lose the original thesis in a stream of posts. Creators generate attention but have weak tools to prove performance, package repeatable strategies, or participate in the economic value they create.

TabAlpha closes this loop. A creator publishes a Tab containing a plain-English thesis, eligible assets, allocation rules, risk limits, and update logic. Users can inspect its complete history, simulate it, follow it, and—where legally and technically available—execute or mirror its current composition from a connected wallet. The protocol records updates and outcomes, creating an auditable reputation graph instead of a screenshot-driven attention market.

The long-term vision is to become the open strategy network for tokenized markets: a distribution, reputation, and execution layer where every credible market idea can become a transparent financial product.

## 2. The Opportunity

Robinhood Chain is being developed as an Ethereum-compatible Layer 2 designed around tokenized real-world and digital assets. Its strategic advantage is not simply lower-cost blockspace; it is the potential convergence of a large retail distribution brand, always-on markets, self-custody, and programmable asset infrastructure.

Three structural trends make TabAlpha timely:

1. **Market content is becoming the discovery layer.** Investors increasingly begin with creators, communities, clips, and live commentary rather than research terminals.
2. **Tokenized assets make portfolios programmable.** Once eligible equities, funds, commodities, and crypto assets share compatible rails, allocation logic can become composable software.
3. **Proof is replacing screenshots.** Onchain history can establish whether a thesis was published before a move, how it changed, and what followers actually experienced.

TABStocks demonstrates a particularly effective early pattern on Robinhood Chain: take a familiar market belief, make it visual and participatory, then settle outcomes through transparent contracts. TabAlpha expands that insight from a single game mechanic into a durable strategy and creator economy.

## 3. Problem

### For investors

- Market ideas are fragmented across social posts, group chats, charts, and brokerage screens.
- Following a thesis requires repetitive manual work and introduces timing and allocation errors.
- Performance claims are difficult to verify and are often selectively presented.
- Traditional copy trading products feel opaque, custodial, and optimized for volume rather than user understanding.

### For creators

- High-quality research is monetized through advertising, sponsorship, or generic subscriptions rather than productized conviction.
- Creators cannot easily publish an auditable strategy history or show how a thesis evolved.
- Existing monetization disconnects creator upside from follower outcomes.
- Communities lack a shared, interactive object around which to discuss and act.

### For the ecosystem

- Tokenized assets need discovery and distribution, not only issuance and liquidity.
- New users need a comprehensible reason to connect a wallet and transact onchain.
- Protocols need higher-quality intent signals than short-term speculative flow.

## 4. Product

### 4.1 The Tab

A Tab is a versioned, rules-based market thesis with six components:

- **Thesis:** a concise explanation of the opportunity and invalidation conditions.
- **Universe:** the eligible assets the Tab may hold.
- **Weights:** current target allocation and maximum position limits.
- **Rules:** rebalance cadence, entry conditions, exits, and risk controls.
- **Proof:** timestamped changes, simulated history, and follower experience.
- **Access:** public, subscriber-only, community-gated, or private.

Examples include “AI Infrastructure,” “US Power Grid,” “Rate-Cut Beneficiaries,” “Founder-Led Compounders,” and event-driven Tabs tied to earnings or macro releases.

### 4.2 User journey

1. Discover a Tab through the feed, a creator link, or an embedded card on X.
2. Read the thesis and inspect current weights, risk, and version history.
3. Run a simulation and choose a fixed-dollar or percentage allocation.
4. Connect an EVM wallet and approve eligible actions.
5. Receive rebalance alerts or opt into bounded automated execution.
6. Track results against the creator’s published objective and risk budget.

### 4.3 Creator journey

1. Connect a wallet and create a public identity.
2. Build a Tab from eligible Robinhood Chain assets.
3. Define the thesis, allocation constraints, rebalance rules, and fee model.
4. Publish a signed version with an immutable timestamp.
5. Distribute a live card to X, communities, and partner applications.
6. Earn subscription, creator, and performance-aligned fees under clear disclosures.

### 4.4 Product surfaces

- **Discovery Feed:** ranked Tabs based on relevance, evidence quality, consistency, and risk-adjusted outcomes.
- **Tab Studio:** no-code strategy creation, simulation, and publishing.
- **Execution Router:** quotes and transaction preparation across eligible Robinhood Chain venues.
- **Proof Profile:** creator history, version changes, realized outcomes, and disclosure record.
- **Embeddable Cards:** live strategy previews for X, newsletters, and partner apps.
- **Alpha API:** strategy metadata and intent endpoints for wallets, aggregators, and agents.

## 5. The Closed-Loop Flywheel

1. Creators publish useful Tabs.
2. Tabs create shareable, real-time market content.
3. Content attracts users with clear investment intent.
4. Users connect wallets, follow Tabs, and generate execution volume.
5. Activity produces verifiable outcome data.
6. Outcome data improves ranking and creator reputation.
7. Better discovery increases creator earnings and attracts stronger creators.
8. More creators expand asset demand, liquidity, and protocol revenue.

The defensibility is not a static interface. It is the combined graph of theses, versions, creator identities, follower behavior, and realized outcomes.

### Supply side: market creators

Independent analysts, finance creators, themed portfolio builders, community operators, and research collectives with an audience but limited product infrastructure.

### Expansion: agents and financial applications

Wallets, trading interfaces, research products, and AI agents that need structured strategy data, signed intent, and execution-ready portfolio objects.

## 8. Business Model

### Phase 1: discovery and creator tools

- Free public Tabs and simulations.
- Pro creator subscription for advanced analytics, custom branding, exports, and deeper audience intelligence.
- Paid Tab subscriptions with platform revenue share.

### Phase 2: execution

- Transparent routing or interface fee on eligible executed volume.
- Creator fee on followed volume, disclosed before confirmation.
- Partner revenue from embedded distribution and wallet integrations.

### Phase 3: network services

- Alpha API usage plans for applications and agents.
- Sponsored discovery slots clearly separated from organic rankings.
- Compliance, analytics, and white-label infrastructure for professional publishers.

### Illustrative take rate architecture

- Creator subscription: creator sets price; TabAlpha retains 15%.
- Execution interface: 5–15 basis points depending on asset and venue economics.
- Creator execution share: optional 20–40% of TabAlpha’s net interface fee.
- API: usage-based with free developer tier and paid production plans.

All percentages are planning assumptions and require legal, market, and unit-economic validation before launch.

## 9. Go-to-Market

### Wedge: “The market, one Tab at a time”

Launch with a small group of native market creators and a recurring public format: each Tab is a concise, visual thesis with a live composition and proof timeline. This makes every product object a distribution object.

### Launch sequence

1. **Founding 20 creators:** recruit opinionated creators across equities, macro, crypto, and sector themes.
2. **Genesis Tabs:** publish 50 high-quality public Tabs before opening user creation.
3. **Weekly Tab Battles:** pair opposing theses and let users follow, simulate, and discuss both sides without forcing binary gambling.
4. **Proof Season:** run a 30-day public cohort with fixed rules and a visible leaderboard based on risk-adjusted consistency, not raw returns.
5. **Execution unlock:** gradually enable wallet actions for eligible assets after audits and legal review.

### Distribution channels

- X-native live cards and short videos.
- Creator referral links with transparent attribution.
- Robinhood Chain ecosystem partnerships and hackathons.
- Wallet and trading-interface embeds.
- SEO pages for durable themes and historical Tab versions.
- Data-driven weekly email: “Tabs moving before the market.”

## 10. Token Strategy

TabAlpha should not launch a liquid token at the beginning. A premature token would attract mercenary volume, complicate compliance, and obscure product-market fit.

The first network primitive should be non-transferable **Proof Points** earned for useful behavior: publishing complete theses, maintaining strategies, identifying invalidation events, and generating sustained follower value. Points may influence access, discovery, and future governance eligibility but should make no promise of financial value.

Only after durable usage should the team evaluate a token for protocol governance, staking against creator integrity, fee alignment, and ecosystem incentives. Any decision requires jurisdiction-specific legal analysis.

## 11. Technology Architecture

### Front end

- Vue 3 and Vite.
- Wallet connection through the EIP-1193 provider standard.
- Responsive, accessible, English-first interface.

### Onchain components

- Tab registry for signed metadata hashes and version timestamps.
- Optional vault or intent contracts for bounded execution.
- Fee splitter for transparent creator and protocol revenue.
- Attestation layer for disclosures and creator verification.

### Offchain components

- Indexed market and strategy metadata.
- Simulation and risk engine.
- Ranking and recommendation service.
- Notification and social-card rendering service.
- Compliance policy engine controlling asset and feature availability by jurisdiction.

### Security principles

- Non-custodial by default.
- Explicit transaction previews and spending bounds.
- No unlimited token approvals in the standard flow.
- Independent smart-contract audits before value-bearing launch.
- Pausable execution adapters without freezing user-owned assets.
- Public incident response and bug bounty programs.

## 12. Compliance and Trust

TabAlpha must be designed with legal boundaries as a product constraint, not a footer disclaimer.

- Separate publishing tools, simulations, and transaction interfaces.
- Provide clear compensation and conflict disclosures for every creator.
- Prohibit undisclosed paid promotion, wash activity, and manipulated history.
- Use jurisdiction-aware access controls for tokenized securities and restricted assets.
- Avoid claims of guaranteed returns or “risk-free alpha.”
- Evaluate broker-dealer, investment adviser, exchange, money transmission, and financial-promotion implications with qualified counsel in each target market.
- Preserve an immutable audit trail while supporting legally required privacy controls for offchain data.

## 17. Principal Risks and Mitigation

- **Network timing:** maintain an EVM-compatible architecture and a valuable read/simulation product before execution launches.
- **Asset availability:** begin with clearly eligible assets and treat universe expansion as a policy decision.
- **Creator manipulation:** signed disclosures, delayed edits, version history, surveillance, and meaningful ranking penalties.
- **Regulatory classification:** modularize publishing, advice-like features, subscriptions, and execution; obtain counsel before each unlock.
- **Bad performance:** rank consistency, process, and risk disclosure rather than celebrating only raw winners.
- **Smart-contract risk:** minimize custody, cap permissions, audit contracts, and stage transaction limits.
- **Cold-start risk:** launch with curated Genesis Tabs rather than an empty marketplace.

## 18. The Investment Case

TabAlpha sits at the intersection of three compounding networks: financial creators, programmable assets, and verifiable onchain identity. The initial product is understandable—a market idea packaged as a live Tab—but the platform can expand into the reputation and intent layer through which users, wallets, and agents discover and act on tokenized markets.

If Robinhood Chain becomes a meaningful rail for tokenized finance, the winning application may not be the interface with the most charts. It may be the network that tells users what is worth opening, why it matters, who has earned trust, and how to act without leaving the thesis behind. That is the position TabAlpha is designed to own.
